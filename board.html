President

The president of the Oakland Tech Hiking Club is responsible for leading meetings.

Vice President

The vice president of the Oakland Tech Hiking Club assists the president and helps organize hiking trips.

Secretary

The secretary of the Oakland Tech Hiking Club is responsible for taking minutes during meetings and maintaining club records.

Historian

The historian of the Oakland Tech Hiking Club is responsible for documenting the club's history and events.

Treasurer

The treasurer of the Oakland Tech Hiking Club is responsible for managing the club's finances and ensuring proper budgeting.

Photographer

The photographer of the Oakland Tech Hiking Club is responsible for capturing images during hiking trips and club events.
